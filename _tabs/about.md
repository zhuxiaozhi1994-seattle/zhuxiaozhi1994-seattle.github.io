## Biography

Hi! My name is **Xiaozhi (Alan) Zhu** and I am currently a Research Scientist at **Meta Platforms, Inc.** (Meta Superintelligence Lab).

Before joining Meta, I worked as an Applied Scientist at **Amazon** and obtained my Ph.D. in [Applied and Computational Mathematics and Statistics (ACMS) from the University of Notre Dame](https://acms.nd.edu/), advised by [Prof. Yong-Tao Zhang](https://www3.nd.edu/~yzhang10/). At Notre Dame, I worked on sparse grid methods for PDEs and surrogate models for fluid dynamics simulations.

My current research interests focus on **3D shape generation**, **Vision-Language Models (VLMs)**, **world models**, **self-supervised learning**, and **reinforcement learning**.
